# Quality Scoring Rubric

Score this article from 0-100 across these weighted criteria:

- Technical accuracy and depth (35%): are claims correct, specific, and non-generic?
- Structural richness (25%): diagrams present and meaningfully placed, code blocks correct and
  runnable-looking, comparison table present, and the article is substantive (1200+ words). An
  article under 1000 words cannot score above 60 on this criterion regardless of other qualities.
- Clarity and flow (20%): logical progression, scannable subheadings, no redundant filler.
- Originality of angle (20%): does this say something more specific than a generic tutorial would?

{{article}}

Output `score`, `reasoning` (2-3 sentences), `strengths` (2-4 bullets), `weaknesses` (2-4 bullets).
Be a harsh grader — a competent-but-generic article should score 50-65, not 85+.
